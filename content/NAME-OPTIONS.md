# Firm name options for the personal injury firm

Superseded on 31 October 2026: the firm name is 10flat (domain 10flatlaw.com). Kept for the record.

## What Tim said about the name (Zoom, 29 October 2026, 26:40 to 27:25)

- "Both are gonna be branded." Not a partner's surname.
- His own examples of the branded style: "Southwest Injury Attorneys", "Shepherd's attorney".
- "Something sounds corporate and global. Okay, we're not going to use the word global, obviously."

## What Tim said about the firm, which the name can carry

- "Let's kill everybody on price." "Half the price of what other attorneys would charge." "Disrupt the whole industry."
- "Hundreds and hundreds of cases." Volume, not boutique.
- "The client doesn't do much." Simple intake: what happened, police report, file the claim.
- AI does the work, the attorney oversees. The name should not lean on "AI"; the regulatory check on AI-led intake is still open.

## Rules applied to every option

- No surname. No "global". No "win", "best", "top" or anything that promises a result (bar advertising rules).
- No "Attorneys" or "Associates" in the plural while there is one attorney of record; several states treat that as misleading. "Injury Law" or "Law" is safer.
- No "cheap" or "discount"; low price is the pitch on the page, not in the name.
- Availability (trademark, domain, existing firms) is unchecked for all of them.

## Recommendation

1. **Clearfee Law** if the name should sell the one new thing: published fees.
2. **Meridian Injury Law** if Tim wants the corporate, global feel he described and is happy to let the website carry the price message.
3. **Upfront Injury Law** as the middle ground: corporate enough, and the price promise is implied.

Before any name is used: trademark search, domain check, a search for existing firms with the same name in the chosen state, and Azure's confirmation that the state allows trade names for law firms and does not require the attorney's name in it.
