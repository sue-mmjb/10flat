Deadlines to know

  • Texas injury lawsuits generally must be filed within two years of the crash.
  • Some records, such as driver logs and truck data, may be kept only for a limited time, so it helps to start early.
  • If a government vehicle was involved, a written notice can be due within six months, and some cities set shorter deadlines.

General information, not legal advice. Your attorney checks the deadlines for your case first.

Questions people ask

What is the attorney fee?

10% of the total amount recovered, and it stays 10% even if we go to trial. Case costs and liens are paid from your share after the fee. A one-third fee is common for Texas injury cases.

What do I pay if we lose?

Nothing. If there is no recovery, you owe no fee and no case costs. We advance the case costs while your case is open.

What are case costs, and who pays my medical bills and liens?

Case costs are money spent on your case, such as medical records, police reports, filing fees and expert reports. We advance them, and they are repaid from your share of the recovery at exactly what they cost, with no markup. Medical bills and liens (money owed to doctors, hospitals or your health insurer) are also paid from your share, the same way at any firm. If there is no recovery, you owe no fee and no case costs.
